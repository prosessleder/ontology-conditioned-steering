# Literature-search audit

**Review date and cutoff:** 9 October 2026, America/Los_Angeles. This is an adversarial, targeted review, not a systematic review or an exhaustive originality certification. The searches sought work that could defeat the assignment's broad novelty claims and distinguish the specific proposed protocol from existing methods. They were run through the web search/browser tool; primary manuscripts and official publication records supplied the evidence. Search rankings, indexing delay, access controls, and future metadata corrections limit reproducibility.

The query groups below preserve the actual query strings used during discovery. They are grouped by search call, not by importance. Searches were followed by targeted primary-page opens and citation/metadata checks. No result-count, deduplication-count, or PRISMA flow is asserted: the tool did not provide a complete exported search corpus. IDs identify retained sources in [references.md](../references.md); the adversarial interpretation is in [related-work.md](related-work.md).

## Handling discovery-only and inaccessible evidence

Secondary search results, social discussions, and summaries helped discover sources, but did not supply scientific result claims. A secondary page names authors and a venue for SPIKE; the citation intentionally remains anonymous because the primary final identity and decision were not verified. R32 is used as a novelty threat at the level of its accessible indexed primary method description, not as an accepted or replicated result. Author-hosted ontology proposals likewise defeat a broad first-idea claim without constituting experimental support for effectiveness.

Generic web ontology manifests and informal fantasy-generation examples were not treated as evidence of an evaluated activation-steering method. Some newer steering metrics, nonlinear variants, and review articles were surfaced without complete tracing; no comprehensive census of them is claimed. Common terminology and citation indexes can hide an exact precedent under a different label. Searches in additional languages, forward/backward citation tracing by independent experts, and final metadata resolution are warranted before stronger publication claims.

## Bounded audit decision

The reviewed evidence defeats claims of a new contrastive algorithm, first conditional control, first ontology-related direction, first use of structured knowledge for hidden-state updates, first mitigation of reasoning content effects, and first fictional-world evaluator. It does not establish an exact prior instance of the full proposed combination: executable multi-action causal planning, distinct unknown/prohibited/permitted statuses, local rewind changing from unknown to narrowly permitted, and stale-vector harm tests. That remaining gap is a candidate protocol contribution, not proof of originality. The manuscript must disclose that model experiments remain pending.
